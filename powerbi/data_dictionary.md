## 6. Analytical Integrity & Data Boundaries

1. **Unmeasured Variables (Unavailable in Dataset):**
   - **Promised Delivery Time (SLA):** The dataset does *not* record customer estimated promise times or contract ETAs. Therefore, deliveries cannot be strictly labeled "Late" against customer promises; instead, deliveries are analyzed against objective duration benchmarks (e.g. `> 30 min Threshold` or dataset `delivery_speed = 'Slow'`).
   - **Kitchen Prep Latency:** Restaurant food cooking time is not captured separately from transit time (`Time_Orderd` to `Time_Order_picked` is missing in some rows and includes courier transit to merchant).
   - **Customer Cancellation Reasons:** No cancelled orders exist in this dataset; all records represent completed deliveries.
2. **Correlation vs. Causation:**
   - Weather and traffic show strong correlation with trip duration, but local road infrastructure, elevation, and rider queue density during storms also contribute to variance.
